# Overview of Customer Portal

## Purpose

The Customer Portal allows registered customers to view support requests, download approved product documentation, and update their contact information.

## Intended audience

This document is intended for:

- Customers with an active account
- Customer support representatives
- Account administrators

## Key features

### Support requests

Customers can create a support request, add comments, and review the current request status.

### Document downloads

Customers can download product guides and release notes that are available for their account.

### Profile management

Customers can update their display name, telephone number, and communication preferences.

## Access requirements

Users must have:

1. An active customer account
2. A verified email address
3. A supported web browser
